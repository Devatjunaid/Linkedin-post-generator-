## Full description
BuildMate is a construction calculator and material estimator for builders, contractors and DIY home renovators. Enter your measurements in feet and inches or in metres, and BuildMate tells you exactly how much concrete, brick, block, cement, mortar, rebar, tile, grout and paint to buy, and what it will cost in your state or country.

Unlike basic calculators, BuildMate uses real product sizes from where you build: US modular bricks and CMU blocks, UK and European brick formats, 94 lb or 25 kg cement bags, quarts, gallons and litre paint cans, #3–#8 or Ø6–Ø25 rebar. It covers all 50 US states plus DC and 31 European countries, with local sales tax or VAT, currency and units.

BRICK & BLOCK CALCULATOR
• Bricks per square foot or m² for 23 real brick and block sizes (modular, queen, king, CMU, NF, DF, AAC and more)
• Doors and windows deducted automatically
• Single or double wythe, veneer wall ties, wastage and pallets
• Mortar calculator: cement bags and sand from the actual mortar volume
• To-scale wall drawing so you can check your numbers

CONCRETE & CEMENT CALCULATOR
• Slabs, footings, walls, round and square columns, round pads and steps
• Volume in cubic yards, cubic feet or m³
• US psi or EU strength classes (C16/20 to C35/45)
• Cement bags, sand, gravel and water for mixing yourself
• Pre-mixed concrete bags (40, 50, 60, 80 lb or 25 kg)
• Ready-mix concrete order rounded the way plants sell it, with the number of trucks and a short-load warning
• Recommends bags or truck for your job size

REBAR & STEEL CALCULATOR
• Rebar mesh for slabs: bar count each way from spacing and cover
• Lap splices when bars are longer than stock length
• Cutting plan that reduces offcut waste, steel weight in lb or kg
• Tie wire and rebar chairs

TILE & FLOORING CALCULATOR
• Exact row-by-column count for straight lay
• Extra cutting waste for brick offset, diagonal and herringbone patterns
• Boxes to buy, spare tiles, tile adhesive by trowel notch and grout by joint size
• Plan view of the floor pattern

PAINT CALCULATOR
• Room or wall area, doors and windows, number of coats, ceiling and primer
• Surface type: smooth, textured, masonry or new drywall
• Finds the cheapest mix of paint cans (gallons, quarts or litres) and shows the leftover

COST ESTIMATE & SHOPPING LIST
• Save calculations into projects and build a complete material list
• Sales tax with your local rate, or VAT shown as included
• Contingency, delivery fee and budget tracker
• Tick off materials you already have
• Export a PDF quote or CSV, or share a text list with your contractor or supplier

MORE
• Edit every price to match your supplier; costs update instantly
• Unit converter: feet ⇄ metres, square feet ⇄ m², cubic yards ⇄ m³, psi ⇄ MPa, lb ⇄ kg, gallons ⇄ litres
• 4 themes, including Hi-Vis for bright job sites, and smooth material animations
• Works offline, no account needed

Whether you're pouring a patio slab, building a brick wall, tiling a bathroom or painting a room, BuildMate helps you buy the right amount of material, avoid extra trips to the store and keep your project on budget.

Note: BuildMate gives estimates based on standard trade formulas and the prices you enter. Starting prices are samples. Always confirm quantities with your supplier and follow your local building codes or engineer's drawings.
