# Privacy Policy – BuildMate: Material Calculator

Last updated: [DATE]

This privacy policy explains how the BuildMate Android app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is published by [DEVELOPER / COMPANY NAME]. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 1. Summary
- You do not need an account to use the app.
- Your projects, measurements, prices and settings are stored **only on your device**. We do not have servers that receive them.
- The app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use some device information, as described below and in Google's own policies.

## 2. Information stored on your device
The app saves the following locally so it can work offline:
- Projects, estimate line items and notes you create
- Prices you enter, your chosen region, units, theme and animation setting

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or export it (for example, sharing a PDF or CSV estimate through another app). If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your Google account backup under Google's terms. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial and native ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
- Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
- Approximate location derived from your IP address
- Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
- Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic or crash data related to ads

Google processes this information as described in its policies:
- How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell personal information, and we do not receive your name, email address or precise location from AdMob.

## 4. Consent and your choices
- **Europe, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the app shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**.
- **US states with privacy laws:** where required, the app shows Google's US state privacy messaging and honours opt-out choices.
- **Personalised ads:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## 5. Permissions
The app requests internet and network-state access only, to load ads and consent forms. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files. Exported files are created in the app's private cache and shared only when you choose.

## 6. Children
The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

## 7. Data security and retention
Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. We keep no copy of your data. Google retains ad-related data according to its own policies.

Because we don't hold your data, the quickest way to delete app data is to uninstall the app. For data processed by Google, use the links above or contact Google.
