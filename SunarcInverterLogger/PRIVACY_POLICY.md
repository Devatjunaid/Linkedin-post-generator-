## What stays on your device

- **Inverter photos and screenshots** you scan are processed on your phone by Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. They are not uploaded. A small copy of each scanned photo is kept in the app's private storage so you can check it later; it is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Readings, system details, prices and your optional location** (used only to work out sunrise, sunset and expected output) are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Backups and exports** (CSV, PDF, JSON) are created only when you ask, and saved or shared wherever you choose.
- **Local network reads:** if you enter your inverter's IP address, the app connects directly to the inverter on your home network. No data passes through our servers; we do not run any.

We do not collect, sell or share your readings, photos or location.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to photograph your inverter display. Only used while the scanner is open.
- **Notifications:** only if you turn on the sunset reminder or monthly summary.
- **Internet / network state:** for ads and for optional reads of your inverter on your home network.
