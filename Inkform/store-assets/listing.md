## Full description (4000 max)
Inkform is a fast, private PDF form filler and signature app. Fill and sign PDF forms right on your phone – fill in fields, add your electronic signature and save a finished PDF in seconds. Everything works offline: your documents never leave your device.

FILL PDF FORMS IN SECONDS
• Open any PDF form from your files, email or chat apps
• Fillable fields are detected automatically: text boxes, checkboxes, radio buttons and drop-down lists
• Guided "Next field" mode with a progress bar, so you never miss a field
• Auto-fill your name, email, phone and address from your saved details
• No fillable fields? Tap anywhere to add text, today's date, ticks ✓ and crosses ✗

SIGN DOCUMENTS WITH YOUR E-SIGNATURE
• Draw your signature with your finger or stylus
• Type your name in a handwriting style, or import a photo of your signature – the paper background is removed automatically
• Save signatures and initials and sign PDFs with one tap
• Drag to place and pinch to resize your signature on contracts, agreements, invoices and forms

SCAN TO PDF
• Scan paper documents with your camera and turn them into a PDF
• Convert photos to PDF – pages are auto-rotated and fitted to A4

SAVE, PROTECT & SHARE
• Flatten form fields so values can't be changed
• Password protect PDFs with AES-256 encryption
• Remove hidden metadata before sending
• Save to your phone, share by email or chat, or print
• Revert to the original document at any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works 100% offline – no sign-up, no account, no cloud uploads
• Your PDFs, signatures and personal details stay on your device

MADE FOR EVERYDAY PAPERWORK
Rental and job applications, tax forms, contracts, NDAs, invoices, school permission slips, medical forms, consent forms and more. Inkform is a simple document signer and PDF filler for work, school and home.

Choose from 4 beautiful themes – Citrus, Midnight, Coral and Violet – and enjoy a smooth, modern design.

Inkform is free and supported by ads.

Note: Inkform adds visual electronic signatures to PDFs. It does not create certificate-based digital signatures.
