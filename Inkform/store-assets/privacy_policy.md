This privacy policy explains how the Inkform Android app ("Inkform", "the app", "we") handles information. Inkform is built to work offline: your documents are processed on your device.

## 1. Information you create in the app (stays on your device)

- **Documents (PDFs)** that you open, scan, fill or sign
- **Signatures and initials** that you draw, type or import
- **"My details"** (name, email, phone, address, date of birth, company) that you choose to save for auto-fill
- **Settings** such as your chosen theme

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload, collect, sell or share it. We have no servers that receive your documents and there is no user account. App backups to the cloud and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this data. It is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

When you choose to save, share or print a document, it is sent only to the place you choose (for example a folder, an email app or a printer). That app handles it under its own privacy policy.

## 2. Permissions and device features

- **Internet and network state** are used only to load and show ads (see section 3).
- **Files:** documents are opened and saved through Android's system file picker. Inkform only sees files you choose.
- **Photos:** photos are selected through Android's photo picker. Inkform only sees the photos you choose.
- **Camera:** scans use your device's camera app. Inkform does not request camera permission and only receives the photos you take.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Inkform is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- information about ad interactions and app performance (diagnostics)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Consent:** where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**.

**Opt out of personalised ads:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## 4. Children

Inkform is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Security

Your documents and signatures stay in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. When you export a PDF with password protection, it is encrypted with AES-256. We never store or see your password.

## 6. Your choices and rights

All of your content is under your control on your device: you can view, edit and delete documents, signatures and "My details" in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. For questions about data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls linked above.
