Dot Dash! – Connect the Dots

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

The short version

  • Dot Dash! is a game for children. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no chat.
  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Game progress stays on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. All ad requests are marked as child-directed, so ads are not personalised and are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
  • The app does not use the Android advertising ID, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

1. Who we are

Dot Dash! – Connect the Dots (package name com.dotdash.kids) is developed by [DEVELOPER OR STUDIO NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you or your child use the app, and how. If you have questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We don't ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo, voice or location, and there is no way to create an account or talk to other players.

Information stored only on your device

To remember progress, the app saves a small amount of game data in the device's private app storage:

  • which levels are finished and how many stars were earned
  • stickers collected, colours chosen in "Color it!", Mystery Eggs and daily-gift date
  • sound and music settings

This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Read-aloud numbers

The "Say it" button and spoken picture names use the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device (for example Google's speech services). The app only passes the words to be spoken, such as "13" or "It's a T-Rex!". No personal information is involved.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dot Dash! is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To make ads appropriate for children:

  • Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (GDPR).
  • The maximum ad content rating is set to G (general audiences).
  • Ads are not personalised and are not based on a child's interests, and Google does not use them for remarketing.
  • The app removes the advertising ID permission, so the advertising ID is not used.
  • Google AdMob is one of the ad networks self-certified for Google Play's Families program.

To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (used to estimate country or region), device model and operating system version, app version, and information about ad views and taps. This information is handled by Google under its own policies; we do not receive it in a form that identifies your child. You can read more at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tapping an ad may open a web page or the Play Store outside the app. We recommend that parents supervise young children while they play.

Dot Dash! is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, the GDPR and UK GDPR (including the Age Appropriate Design Code) in Europe and the UK, and similar laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided to us, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't collect any. The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep no user data on our side. Game progress is stored only on the device and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data (Settings › Apps › Dot Dash! › Storage › Clear data). For information Google processes for ads, see Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act). Because we do not hold personal information about you or your child, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. You are always welcome to contact us with any request or question.

9. Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections made by the ad service use encryption (HTTPS).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above, and if the change is significant we will also say so in the app's Play Store listing.
