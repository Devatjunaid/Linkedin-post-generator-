```
Connect the dots and watch the picture come alive! Dot Dash is a fun dot to dot game for kids that turns counting into an adventure. Join the dots 1, 2, 3 in order, and Zip the firefly draws a glowing line. When the last dot is joined, the hidden picture pops out in full color and roars, honks or flies away!

Made for toddlers, preschool and kindergarten kids aged 3 to 8, Dot Dash helps children learn numbers, practice counting and build fine motor skills while they play.

🦖 60 CONNECT THE DOTS PUZZLES IN 3 WORLDS
• Animal Park – lion, elephant, giraffe, penguin, owl, puppy, kitty and more animals
• Turbo Town – race car, fire truck, school bus, train, rocket, airplane, submarine and more vehicles
• Dino Valley – T-Rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Pterodactyl and more dinosaurs
Every level is a new surprise, from easy 10-dot pictures to 25-dot challenges.

🔢 LEARN NUMBERS AND COUNTING
• Count 1 2 3 all the way to 25 as you join the dots in order
• "Say it" reads each number out loud, perfect for kids who are still learning to count
• Every dot plays a musical note, so each picture plays its own little tune
• A gentle hint shows the next dot whenever a child gets stuck

🎉 REWARDS KIDS LOVE
• Win up to 3 stars on every level
• Each finished picture becomes a sticker in the Sticker Book
• Hatch a Mystery Egg every 5 levels
• Open a daily gift for bonus stars
• Collect 150 stars to unlock Golden Zip with a rainbow trail

🎨 COLORING FUN
After the reveal, kids can color the picture with their favorite colors and keep it in the sticker book.

👶 DESIGNED FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big, bright dots that are easy to tap, or simply drag a finger through them
• No timers, no game over and no scary sounds, just a soft "boing" on a wrong tap
• Cheerful sounds, friendly voice and calm background music
• Plays offline, with no Wi-Fi needed for the game itself
• Progress is saved automatically

WHY PARENTS LIKE DOT DASH
Dot to dot puzzles are a classic way to practice number recognition, sequencing, hand-eye coordination and focus. Dot Dash keeps the learning playful, so kids ask to play again and again. Animal, dinosaur and car pictures in one app make it one of those learning games for kids that toddlers and big kids both enjoy. It is a great educational game for car rides, waiting rooms and quiet time at home.

Dot Dash is free to play and contains ads. Ads are set up for children: they are child-directed and limited to general audiences.

If your child loves toddler games, dinosaur games or connect the dots for kids, they will love Dot Dash. Connect the dots, reveal the picture and collect them all. Download Dot Dash and start the adventure!
```

Notes:
- Keep the brand "Dot Dash" first in the title so the app stays recognisable; "Connect the Dots" carries the main search weight.
- Google Play doesn't allow "best", "#1", "free" or emojis in the title, and doesn't allow repeated keyword lists in the description. This copy stays within those rules.
- Category: **Educational** (or Puzzle). Target age groups in the Families program: **5 & under** and **6–8**.
- The description says the app contains ads and that they are child-directed. Keep that line; Families policy expects honesty about ads.
- Upload the gameplay promo video to YouTube and add it as the promo video; it lifts conversion.

### Gaps Dot Dash can win
- Most kids' competitors cover **one** theme (animals *or* dinosaurs *or* vehicles). Dot Dash has all three, so the listing leans on "dinosaurs, animals and cars".
- Few have a modern, game-like feel. Lean on the stars, stickers, Mystery Eggs and the pictures that come alive in the screenshots and video.
- Many are old or paid-to-unlock. Dot Dash has all 60 levels free to play.
