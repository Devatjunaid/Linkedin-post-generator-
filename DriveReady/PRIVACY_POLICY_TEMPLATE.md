# DriveReady — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

DriveReady ("the app") is published by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect
We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, location or contacts.
Your study progress (selected country, test date, scores, checklists) is stored **only on your device** and is
included in Android's own device backup if you have it turned on.

## Advertising
The app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
- app interaction and diagnostic data (for example, ad impressions, clicks and crash information),
- approximate location derived from your IP address.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent
through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any
time in **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**, and you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's
Google settings.

## Text-to-speech
The oral-test feature uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. No audio is recorded.

## Children
The app is intended for learner drivers aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

## Not a government app
DriveReady is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with any government or licensing authority.
