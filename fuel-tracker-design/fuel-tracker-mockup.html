Fuel & Trip Cost Tracker: Screen Layouts

Material 3 layouts for Jetpack Compose. A deep-blue primary carries the actions, green tertiary marks savings and efficiency, and cool neutrals keep the dashboard calm. Each screen is shown in light and dark mode.

UI design v1 · Oct 2026

2–5 · Screens: bottom navigation, calculator, history, ad slot

Layout specs

Navigation

  • Material 3 NavigationBar with two destinations
  • Pill indicator on the selected tab, 80dp tall
  • One top app bar whose title follows the selected tab

Calculator

  • Metric / Imperial segmented toggle swaps the units
  • Vehicle chips pick the vehicle to save the trip under
  • Outlined fields with tonal icons and unit suffixes; decimal keypad
  • Tap the efficiency unit (⇄) to switch between km/L and L/100km
  • Result card updates as you type; Save stays disabled until all three fields are valid

History

  • LazyColumn: a stats header item, then trips grouped by month
  • Card shows vehicle, date, distance, efficiency and total cost
  • 16dp side gutter, 12dp gap between items

Ad placeholder

  • Fixed 50dp banner (320×50) plus 8dp vertical padding = 66dp
  • Sits between the content and the nav bar, in the Scaffold bottomBar
  • The slot is always reserved, so content doesn't jump when an ad loads or fails
