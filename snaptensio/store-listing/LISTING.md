## Full description (2473 / 4000 characters)
```
SnapTensio turns your digital blood pressure monitor into a smart BP log. Point your phone's camera at the monitor's screen and your systolic, diastolic and pulse readings are filled in automatically. No typing, no Bluetooth pairing, no account.

Requires a digital blood pressure monitor (cuff). SnapTensio does not measure blood pressure by itself; it reads and records the numbers your monitor shows.

SCAN YOUR BP MONITOR
• Made for digital upper-arm and wrist monitors with a number display
• Reads SYS, DIA and pulse with on-device text recognition
• Checks several camera frames before accepting a reading
• Torch support for dim rooms, or pick a photo from your gallery
• Check and correct any number before saving

KNOW WHAT YOUR NUMBERS MEAN
• Colour-coded blood pressure ranges: Normal, Elevated, Stage 1, Stage 2
• Choose AHA/ACC 2017 or ESC/ESH guideline ranges
• Set your own target, for example the one your doctor gave you
• Safety guidance for very high readings above 180/120

TRACK TRENDS AND PATTERNS
• Blood pressure charts for 7 days, 30 days, 90 days and 1 year
• Averages, highest and lowest readings, time in each range
• Morning vs evening comparison and pulse tracking
• Simple insights from your own readings, such as "evenings run higher" or "readings after coffee are higher"
• See how readings changed after starting a medication

ACCURATE HOME READINGS
• Guided session: 3 readings, 1 minute apart, averaged automatically
• Checklist for correct measuring position
• Log arm, position, tags (coffee, stress, exercise, poor sleep and more) and notes

REPORTS FOR YOUR DOCTOR
• Clean PDF blood pressure report with chart, averages, range breakdown, medications and every reading
• Export to CSV for spreadsheets or backup
• Import readings from a CSV file

MEDICATION AND READING REMINDERS
• Medication schedule with "mark as taken"
• Weekly adherence and refill alerts
• Morning and evening blood pressure check reminders

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Keep separate logs for several people on one phone, each with their own target

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up and no cloud: your health readings stay on your phone
• Works offline
• Light and dark theme

SnapTensio is free and supported by ads. Ads never receive your health data.

Important: SnapTensio is a personal logbook and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always follow your doctor's advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
```
