# SnapTensio Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

SnapTensio ("the app") is a blood pressure logbook published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Health data stays on your device
- Blood pressure readings, pulse, notes, tags, medications and reminders are stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage.
- The app has **no account and no server**. We never receive your health data.
- Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are **turned off** for this data. To keep a copy, use **Settings → Export all readings**, which creates a CSV file that you choose where to send.
- Reports (PDF/CSV) are created on your phone and shared only when you pick an app to share them with.

## Camera
The camera is used only to read the numbers on your blood pressure monitor's screen. Text recognition (Google ML Kit, bundled in the app) runs **on the device**. Camera frames and photos you pick are not saved or uploaded.

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.

## Advertising
The app shows ads through **Google AdMob** to stay free. AdMob may collect and use:
- Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID
- IP address, approximate location derived from it, and app interaction data with ads
- Diagnostic data

**Your health readings are never shared with AdMob or any other party.** In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Deleting your data
Delete a reading or person inside the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything.

## Not a medical device
SnapTensio is a logbook. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Always follow your doctor's advice.
