Draw to Complete Fun - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Draw to Complete Fun ("the app") is a drawing puzzle game made for children. We care about children's privacy and designed the app to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location. The app has no accounts, no chat and no social features.

Game progress (stars, unlocked levels, sound settings and the daily gift streak) is saved only on the device. It is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA) and as coming from users under the age of consent (GDPR), and ads are limited to the "G" (general audiences) content rating. This means:

  • Ads are not personalised and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • The app does not collect the Android Advertising ID.
  • Google may process limited technical information (for example IP address, device type and operating system) to show an ad, prevent fraud and measure the ad. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded videos are only shown when a player chooses to watch one. Links that leave the app (rating the app, this policy) are behind a grown-up check.

Children

The app is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
