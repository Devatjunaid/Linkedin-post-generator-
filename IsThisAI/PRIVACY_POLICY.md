# Privacy Policy — Is This AI?

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Is This AI? checks images by reading labels stored inside the image file. All checking happens on your device.

**Images.** Images you choose are read only on your device. They are never uploaded, sold or shared.

**History.** For each check, the app saves the file name, the result, the report and a small preview image on your device. This data is excluded from device backups. You can delete single checks or all history inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

**Advertising.** The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app usage information to serve, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you are asked before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under About → "Ad privacy choices". You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
