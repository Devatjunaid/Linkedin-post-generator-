# Renew Alert: Google Play store listing

## App name / title (max 30)
Renew Alert: Subscriptions

## Short description (max 80)
Subscription tracker & bill reminder. Cancel free trials before they charge.

## Full description (max 4000)
Renew Alert is the private subscription tracker that reminds you before every renewal, warns you before a free trial turns into a paid plan, and shows exactly how much your subscriptions cost each month and year. No bank link, no account, no sign-up.

Streaming, music, cloud storage, apps, gym, phone plan, news, software: add each recurring payment in seconds and Renew Alert keeps track of every bill for you.

🔔 RENEWAL REMINDERS & BILL ALERTS
• Get a reminder before each subscription renews: on the day, 1, 2, 3 or 7 days before
• Never pay for a forgotten subscription again
• Weekly summary of upcoming bills
• Quiet, reliable reminders that work offline

⏰ FREE TRIAL TRACKER
• Add free trials with their end date
• Trial alerts 3 days before, 1 day before and on the morning the trial ends
• Countdown for every trial, plus one-tap "Keep it" or "I cancelled it"
• See how much money you saved by cancelling

💰 SUBSCRIPTION MANAGER & SPENDING TRACKER
• Monthly and yearly totals for all your subscriptions
• Set a monthly budget and see when you go over
• Spending by category: entertainment, music, productivity, health and more
• Track price increases with full price history
• Cost per use: find out what each subscription really costs you

📅 RENEWAL CALENDAR
• See every upcoming payment on a monthly calendar
• What's already paid this month and what's still to pay
• Spot the days with the biggest bills

📊 INSIGHTS TO SAVE MONEY
• Find subscriptions you rarely use
• Spot overlapping services you pay for twice
• Get alerted when a subscription raises its price
• 6-month and yearly spending trends

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No bank connection and no account needed
• Your subscription data is stored only on your phone
• Fingerprint, face or PIN app lock
• Hide amounts and block screenshots
• Delete all your data any time

💾 BACKUP & EXPORT
• Encrypted backup file protected with your own password
• Restore on a new phone
• Export to CSV (Excel, Google Sheets) and a PDF report
• Import subscriptions from CSV

✨ MORE FEATURES
• Any currency, with your own exchange rates
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom billing cycles
• 70+ quick-add templates for popular service types
• Pause, cancel and reactivate subscriptions
• Notes for each subscription, like how to cancel
• Home-screen widget with your monthly total and next bills
• Beautiful light and dark themes

WHO IS RENEW ALERT FOR?
Anyone who wants to stop wasting money on subscriptions they forgot about: students, families, freelancers and anyone managing recurring payments, memberships and monthly bills.

Renew Alert is free and supported by ads. Ads never see your subscription data.

Start tracking your subscriptions today and get alerted before every renewal.
