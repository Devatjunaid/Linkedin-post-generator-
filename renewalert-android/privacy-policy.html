Privacy Policy for Renew Alert

Super Dev Labs · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Renew Alert mobile application ("Renew Alert", "the app", "we", "us" or "our") handles information. The app is developed and published by Super Dev Labs ("the developer"). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Renew Alert does not require an account and does not connect to your bank, cards or any payment service.
  • Everything you enter (subscription names, prices, dates, notes, payment labels and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not receive, collect or store it on any server.
  • The app shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described in Section 4.

2. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you record subscriptions and related details, such as name, price, currency, billing cycle, payment dates, category, an optional payment label (for example "Visa •• 4821" or "PayPal") and notes.

  • This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • It is not sent to the developer or any third party.
  • It is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.
  • Do not enter full card numbers, passwords, bank details or other sensitive data. The payment field is only a label to help you recognise a charge.

3. Backups and exports

The app lets you create:

  • Encrypted backup files, protected with a password you choose (AES-256 encryption). The developer cannot read these files or recover your password. If you lose the password, the backup cannot be restored.
  • CSV and PDF exports, which are not encrypted.

These files are created only when you choose, and saved only where you choose (for example your phone storage or a cloud drive you select). Once you share or store them elsewhere, your use of that location is governed by its own provider's terms and privacy policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Renew Alert is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver, personalise (where you allow it) and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data.

Your subscription data is never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Consent: Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where applicable).

Your controls: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Learn more about how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • in the app: Settings → Delete all data;
  • by clearing the app's storage in Android settings; or
  • by uninstalling the app.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's own policies and controls.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including on-device storage, optional app lock, optional screenshot blocking and encrypted backups. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device, screen lock and backup passwords secure.

8. Children's privacy

Renew Alert is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that resulted in data being collected by our advertising partner, contact us and we will help where we can.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because subscription data never leaves your device, you control it directly. For advertising data, use the controls in Section 4 or contact Google. You may also contact us with any request or question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
