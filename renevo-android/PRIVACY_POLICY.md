# Renevo privacy policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Renevo ("the app") is published by [YOUR NAME / COMPANY] ("we"). Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

## The short version

- Renevo has no account and no bank connection.
- The subscriptions, prices, notes and settings you enter are stored **only on your device**. We never receive them.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers to show and measure ads.

## Data you enter

Everything you type into Renevo stays in the app's private storage on your phone. It's excluded from Android
cloud backup. It leaves your device only when **you** export a file (encrypted backup, CSV or PDF) and choose
where to save or share it. Encrypted backups are protected with a password only you know. We can't recover it.

You can delete all data at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

## Advertising

Renevo uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. Google may collect
and process:

- the advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- ad interaction and diagnostic data.

This is used to deliver, personalise (where you consent) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Your subscription
data is never shared with Google or any advertiser. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and
some US states), you're asked for consent first. You can change it in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can
also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state:** only for ads and consent.
- **Notifications:** renewal reminders and trial alerts, created on your device.
- **Biometrics:** optional app lock. Fingerprint and face data stay with Android; the app never sees them.

## Children

Renevo is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).
