# Play Store listing

**App name (30):** Renevo — Subscription Tracker

**Short description (80):** Track subscriptions and free trials privately. No bank link, smart reminders.

**Full description:**

Renevo keeps every subscription in one private place, with no bank link and no account.

★ NEVER MISS A RENEWAL
Get a reminder before each charge. Free-trial alerts 3 days, 1 day and the morning before a trial turns paid.

★ SEE WHAT YOU REALLY SPEND
Monthly and yearly totals, a monthly budget, spending by category and a 6-month trend.

★ SMART SAVING TIPS
Spot subscriptions you rarely use, overlapping services and price increases. Track how much you've saved by cancelling.

★ RENEWAL CALENDAR
See every upcoming charge on a calendar, including trials about to convert.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No bank connection, no sign-up
• Data stays on your phone
• Fingerprint / face lock, hide amounts, block screenshots
• Encrypted backups you control

★ MORE
• Any currency, with your own exchange rates
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly or custom billing cycles
• 70 quick-add templates
• CSV import/export and PDF report
• Home-screen widget
• Light and dark themes

Renevo is free and supported by ads.
