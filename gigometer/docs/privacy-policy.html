Gigometer Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE YOU PUBLISH] · App: Gigometer – Mileage Log (app.gigometer.mileage)

Gigometer is a free mileage log for gig drivers. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored and who it is shared with. In short, your trips stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service collects to show ads.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Location (GPS). Used to measure the distance and route of each trip, including in the background when automatic detection is on. Routes, distances and times are saved in a database on your phone. We do not upload or sell your location.
  • Physical activity. Android/Google Play services tell the app when you start or stop driving, so trips can begin and end automatically. This signal is processed on the device and is not stored or uploaded by Gigometer.
  • Trip details you enter. Business/personal category, gig platform, business purpose, manual trips, and the optional name printed on your reports.

We have no servers and no user accounts, so we cannot see this information. It is excluded from cloud backups. It leaves your phone only when you export a PDF/CSV report or a backup file and choose where to send it.

2. Street address lookup

If "Street addresses" is on (Settings), the app uses Android's built-in geocoding service to turn a trip's start and end coordinates into street addresses. Your device sends those coordinates to the geocoding provider (normally Google) only to get the address back. You can turn this off at any time.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gigometer is free because it shows ads: a banner and native ads inside the app. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and share:

Crash logs, diagnostics and performance dataAnalytics, fraud prevention

Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Permissions

  • Location (incl. "Allow all the time"): measuring trips, and recording them automatically.
  • Physical activity: detecting when you start and stop driving.
  • Notifications: the trip-recording notification, saved-trip and review reminders.
  • Internet: loading ads and looking up street addresses.

5. Data retention and deletion

Trips stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes all app data. We hold no copy.

6. Children

Gigometer is intended for adults who drive for work and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Not tax advice

Deduction amounts are estimates based on IRS standard mileage rates. Please confirm with a tax professional.
