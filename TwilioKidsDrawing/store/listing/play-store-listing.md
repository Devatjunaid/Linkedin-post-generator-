# Google Play Store Listing: Twilio Kids Drawing

Ready to paste into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**.

## App name (30 characters max)

```
Twilio Kids Drawing & Coloring
```
30/30. Keywords: **kids drawing**, **coloring**.

> Google Play doesn't allow emojis, ALL CAPS, or words like "best", "#1", "free" or "top" in the
> app name, so none are used. The name on the phone's home screen stays "Twilio Kids Drawing"
> (`strings.xml`), which is allowed.

```

✏️ FREE DRAWING & DOODLING

• Eraser for quick fixes

🖼️ MY GALLERY

🎉 FUN FOR KIDS

🧠 PLAY AND LEARN
Drawing and coloring games help children practice:
• Fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination

👨‍👩‍👧 FOR PARENTS

Twilio Kids Drawing & Coloring is a coloring book, drawing pad and paint studio in one app for boys and girls, from toddlers and preschool to kindergarten and early school age. Download it and start coloring today!
```

## Other Play Console settings to match

- **Category:** Art & Design. Also opt in to **Teacher Approved** review if eligible.
- **Tags:** Coloring, Drawing, Kids, Educational, Art.
- **Target audience** (Policy → App content → Target audience): **5 & under** and **6–8**.
  This puts the app in the Families program, which the app's ad settings are already built for.
- **Ads:** declare **Yes, my app contains ads**.
- **Data safety:** see `data-safety-form.md`.
- **Privacy policy URL:** host `privacy-policy.html` publicly (GitHub Pages, Google Sites, or your
  website) and paste the link.
