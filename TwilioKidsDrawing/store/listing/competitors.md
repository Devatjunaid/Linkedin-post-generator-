Researched October 2026.

**How this was gathered:** Google Play's website couldn't be opened from the research environment,
so live search rankings weren't available. The list comes from Google Play listings that rank in web
search for the app's main keywords ("kids drawing", "coloring book for kids", "drawing games for kids",
"animal coloring"), plus third-party trackers (AppBrain, Apptopia, Similarweb, App Pricing Lab).
Download numbers are shown where a source reported them; they can be out of date. Check a few
listings yourself in the Play Store before relying on the numbers.

## What the research shows

**1. The title keywords everyone fights for:** "coloring", "drawing", "kids", "games", "color",
"paint", "toddler/baby", "book". Nearly every top app has 2–3 of these in its 30-character name.
Your name now has three: *Kids*, *Drawing*, *Coloring*.

**2. Animals are a real niche.** KiDEO, Tip-Tap, KezengaGames and Bonbon all built whole apps around
animal coloring, and those apps have much smaller download numbers than the big general apps, so it's
easier to rank there. With 39 animals and birds in 6 groups, Twilio competes well on "animal coloring"
and "birds" searches.

**3. The leaders win on content volume.** Bimi Boo has 165–176 pages, Coloring & Learn has 250+,
Yateland has 102. 39 pages is fine for launch, but adding more pages in updates (vehicles, dinosaurs,
unicorns, food) would help the app compete and gives you "new pages!" update notes.

**4. Common selling points in descriptions:** age range (2–6), "works offline / no wifi",
"ad-free", "tap to fill", number of pages, learning benefits (colors, fine motor skills).
Your description uses all of these that are true for the app. It doesn't claim "ad-free", because the
app shows ads.

**5. Ad-free competitors.** RV AppStudios (#1), Bimi Boo and ElePant advertise "no ads". Parents
compare this, and Play shows a "Contains ads" label on your listing. Keeping ads family-friendly and
not too frequent matters for ratings. Consider a cooldown between interstitials (for example, at most
one per 60–90 seconds).
