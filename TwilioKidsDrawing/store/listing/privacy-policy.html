Privacy Policy

Twilio Kids Drawing (Android app com.harrycodess.drawingkids)

Developer: HarryCodess · Effective date: October 11, 2026

Twilio Kids Drawing (the "App") is a drawing and coloring app made for children. This policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used, and the choices parents have. We designed the App to collect as little information as possible.

The short version
  • We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts, or location.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up, no chat, and no in-app purchases.
  • Drawings and coloring progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are set up for children: child-directed, non-personalized, and limited to G-rated content.
  • The App does not use the device's Advertising ID.

1. Information stored on the device

The App stores the following only on the child's device, in the App's private storage:

  • Saved artworks: pictures your child saves to "My Gallery".
  • Coloring progress: which colors were used on each animal page, so a page can be finished later.
  • Settings: whether sounds are turned on or off.

This information is never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when artworks are deleted in the Gallery, when the App's data is cleared, or when the App is uninstalled.

2. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from children or other users. The App has no servers of its own, no analytics, and no crash-reporting tools of its own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects limited technical information from the device, such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device (country or city level);
  • App interactions with ads (for example, app launches, ad views and taps);
  • Diagnostic information (for example, crash logs and performance data);
  • Device and app identifiers such as the app set ID.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. This information is encrypted in transit.

Because the App is made for children, we have configured every ad request to protect them:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as under the age of consent (under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Google then disables personalized advertising (interest-based ads and remarketing) for these requests.
  • Ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) maximum ad content rating.
  • The App removes the Android Advertising ID permission, so the Advertising ID is not collected.
  • Google AdMob takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

Ads may contain links to websites or apps. Parents may wish to supervise young children while they use the App. You can learn more about how Google uses information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files outside the App, or location.

5. Children's privacy

The App is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children cannot share information with other users, because the App has no social features, chat, or user accounts. If you believe your child's information has been collected through the App in a way that is not described here, please contact us and we will work to delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We don't hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove everything the App has saved on the device, delete artworks in "My Gallery", clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App. For information held by Google for advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Security

Artworks and settings are stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Information sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR in the EU/UK or state laws in the U.S.), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information and to object to its processing. Because we don't collect personal information, most requests can be handled by deleting the App's data as described above. For any questions or requests, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's update notes on Google Play.
