# Drip Drop Draw

A physics drawing puzzle for kids, written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Draw lines with your
finger to guide water from the tap into a thirsty cup. The cups have faces: they look at the
water, shout "Wheee!", laugh when they are full and cry a little when they stay empty.

Everything in the game is made in this project. The art is drawn with code, the sounds and the
music are generated by `tools/generate_sounds.py`, and the app icon is a vector drawable. The two
fonts (Fredoka and Gochi Hand) are free Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License; their
licences are in `licenses/`.

## Open and run

1. Open the `DripDropDraw` folder in Android Studio.
2. Let Gradle sync. The project uses AGP 9.4.0, Gradle 9.6.0, built-in Kotlin (Kotlin 2.4.21),
   compileSdk 37, targetSdk 36 and minSdk 26. Gradle JDK 21 is fine.
3. Run the `app` configuration.

Debug builds automatically use Google's **test** ad units. Release builds use your real AdMob
IDs. This keeps your AdMob account safe while you test.

Run the tests with `./gradlew test`. They play all 100 levels with their built-in solution
(and with the solution drawn slightly off) and check that none can be won without drawing.

## The game

- **100 levels in 5 worlds** (Kitchen, Garden, Bathtub, Lab, Space). The level map only shows
  worlds the player has reached, and nothing tells the player how many levels there are.
- **New ideas arrive slowly**: walls, fire that turns water to steam, balls, two cups, hanging
  walls you have to draw under, deflectors, seesaws, two-line zig-zags, fans, moving platforms,
  spinners, lids, two taps, moving cups. Space has low gravity.
- **Difficulty grows gently**: a bit less ink, a higher fill line, smaller cups, less spare
  water and more clutter on later levels. Every level was checked to be solvable.
- **Stars** depend on how much ink you use. **Hints** show a dotted solution (free on the first
  3 levels, 30 coins after that). There is no game over: Retry is always one tap away.
- **Animated splash screen**: the title bounces in while a pencil draws a line and water fills
  the cup. Tap to skip.
- **Exit popup** on the main menu's Back press, with **Cancel**, **Rate us** and **Exit**.

## Ads (AdMob)

| Ad | Where | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Banner (adaptive) | Bottom of the menu, level map and game | Two requests straight away; if both fail, it waits 15 s before each new request. Its space is reserved so the game never jumps. |
| Interstitial | Only at breaks: **Next**, **Replay**, **Retry**, and leaving a level | At most one every 30 s. None in the first 30 s after launch or on levels 1–3. Never during play. |
| Rewarded | **Bonus chest** after every 5 finished levels | Always the child's choice ("Watch video" / "No thanks"); gives 50 coins. It only shows when a video is ready. |

IDs are in `app/src/main/java/.../ads/AdsManager.kt` and the App ID is in
`AndroidManifest.xml`.

## Google Play Families checklist

The code already does this:

- Every ad request is tagged **child-directed** and **under the age of consent**, with the max
  ad rating **G**.
- The **advertising ID permission is removed** (`tools:node="remove"` in the manifest), and
  automatic measurement is delayed.
- No analytics, accounts, chat, location or in-app purchases. Progress stays on the device.
- **Rate us** and the **For parents** info are behind a simple grown-up check (a times-table
  question).
- Full-screen ads only appear between levels, and rewarded videos are always opt-in.

You still need to do this in the consoles:

1. **AdMob**: in the app settings, turn on the child-directed setting for the app and choose a
   G maximum ad rating. Don't add mediation networks that aren't Families self-certified.
2. **Play Console > App content**:
   - Target audience: choose the child age groups (for example 5 & under, 6–8 and 9–12).
   - Ads: "Yes, my app contains ads".
   - Privacy policy: add a URL. It must mention AdMob and that ads are child-directed.
   - Data safety: declare what the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects (device or other IDs,
     app interactions, diagnostics, approximate location from IP) for advertising.
   - Content rating: fill in the questionnaire (no violence, no user content).
3. Join the Designed for Families program when Play asks.

## Store assets

`store/` holds a 512×512 icon, a 1024×500 feature graphic and phone screenshots of the real game
screens. The screenshots have a grey bar where the banner ad goes.

## Project map

```
app/src/main/java/com/devatjunaid/dripdropdraw/
  engine/   physics (water particles, balls, seesaws, fans, fire), level generator, game rules
  ui/       Compose screens, game renderer, cup character, logo, dialogs, icons
  ads/      AdMob banner, interstitial and rewarded logic
  audio/    sound effects (SoundPool) and the music loop
  data/     saved progress (stars, coins, settings)
tools/generate_sounds.py   makes every sound in res/raw
```
