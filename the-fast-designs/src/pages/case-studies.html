Case study 01

Nimbus Pay: a dashboard that doubled sign‑ups

Client
Nimbus Pay
Industry
Fintech / Payments
Services
UX, UI, Web Development
Timeline
7 weeks

1The challenge

Nimbus Pay’s product was powerful, but its onboarding was confusing and its marketing site took over five seconds to load on mobile. Trial users dropped off before they ever saw the value — and paid ads were burning budget on a slow landing page.

2Our approach

  • Analysed 1,200 session recordings to find onboarding friction
  • Reduced sign‑up from 9 steps to 4
  • Designed a clean, data‑rich dashboard system with 60+ components
  • Rebuilt the marketing site in Next.js on the edge

3The solution

A fast, trustworthy brand presence paired with a dashboard that surfaces the metrics finance teams care about most. Real‑time charts, smart empty states and contextual tips guide new users to their first payment in minutes, not days.

Case study 02

Maison Lune: luxury e‑commerce that sells

Client
Maison Lune
Industry
Fashion & Apparel
Services
E‑commerce, Art Direction
Timeline
9 weeks

1The challenge

Maison Lune’s clothing is made to last — but its off‑the‑shelf theme felt anything but premium. 72% of traffic came from mobile, yet mobile conversion sat below 0.9%, and slow product pages undermined the brand’s luxury positioning.

2Our approach

  • Art‑directed an editorial, image‑led shopping experience
  • Designed a thumb‑friendly mobile navigation and sticky cart
  • Built a custom Shopify 2.0 theme with lazy‑loaded media
  • Added “complete the look” bundles to raise order value

3The solution

A bespoke storefront that feels like flipping through a fashion magazine — but loads like an app. Rich storytelling on collection pages, one‑tap checkout with Shop Pay and Apple Pay, and smart cross‑sells turned browsers into loyal customers.

0Growth in mobile revenue
0Increase in average order value

Case study 03

Voltline: SEO‑first website for EV charging

Client
Voltline
Industry
Clean Energy / EV
Services
Web Design, SEO, Content
Timeline
6 weeks + ongoing

1The challenge

Voltline was expanding into new cities but was invisible on Google. Its old site was a single page with no location content, a poor Core Web Vitals score and no way to capture fleet and property‑owner leads.

2Our approach

  • Keyword research across 40+ target cities
  • Scalable location‑page templates with live charger data
  • Astro + Sanity CMS for sub‑second page loads
  • Lead funnels for fleets, hosts and drivers

3The solution

A content‑rich, lightning‑fast platform that ranks for “EV charging near me” searches city by city. Structured data, internal linking and a monthly content programme compound traffic growth month after month.
