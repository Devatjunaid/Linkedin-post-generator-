FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Talk to us

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted on its scope. Most marketing websites fall between a focused 5‑page launch site and a 20+ page custom build, while e‑commerce and web apps are priced on features. Fill in our quote form and you’ll receive a fixed, itemised proposal within 48 hours.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites launch in 4–8 weeks. Larger e‑commerce stores and custom web applications typically take 8–14 weeks. You’ll get a detailed timeline before we start.

Will my website be SEO‑friendly and mobile‑responsive?

Always. Every site is built mobile‑first with semantic HTML, schema markup, optimised meta tags, clean URLs, XML sitemaps and Core Web Vitals scores in the green.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We build on easy‑to‑use content management systems and record a walkthrough video so your team can edit pages, publish blog posts and add products without touching code.
