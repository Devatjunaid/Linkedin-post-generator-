# Exam Clock — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Exam Clock is built to keep your study data on your phone. This policy explains what the app stores,
what the advertising partner receives, and the choices you have. The same text is available inside the
app under **Settings › Privacy policy**.

## Data stored on your device
Your exams, topics, study plan, focus history, name (optional) and settings are stored only in the app's
private storage on your device. We do not run servers and we never receive this data. It may be included
in your own Android device backup if backup is turned on.

## Advertising
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device
identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad
interaction data. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it at
any time from **Settings › Ad privacy choices**. Google's privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> · How Google uses data from apps:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

## Permissions
- **Internet / network state** — to load ads.
- **Notifications** — for study reminders and the focus timer, only if you allow them.

No access to contacts, camera, microphone, files or precise location is requested.

## Children
Exam Clock is meant for students aged 13 and over. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens.

## Deleting your data
Use **Settings › Delete all data**, or uninstall the app, to remove everything stored by Exam Clock.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
